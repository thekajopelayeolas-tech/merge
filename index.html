How to play
  • Deploy mechs and merge matching tiers to make your army stronger.
  • Click a mech, then click another cell to move, swap, or merge. Each move costs one turn.
  • Press Play Battle to fight the level's enemies.
  • Each enemy you destroy gives you one extra turn.
  • Enemies match your level. Clear every enemy to advance.
  • Clear level 10 to win. If any enemy survives, the run ends.
